Welcome

An elegant refuge at 1,140 m

This 83 sqm apartment blends modern comfort with Savoyard charm. Its 45 sqm terrace, onto which the living room opens, and its authentic wooden Nordic bath offer a unique escape, in a sun-drenched family resort.

Set in the centre of the resort, steps from the shops, activities and the gondola, it offers 3 cosy bedrooms including a master suite with its own bathroom, high-end equipment and refined décor that create a warm, welcoming atmosphere.

Getting here

Access & directions

265 route de Flaine — Domaine du Bois Blanc — 74300 Les Carroz d'Arâches

By car

A40 motorway, exit 19 “Flaine – Les Carroz”, then RN 205 towards Sallanches. At the Super U roundabout in Magland, take the D6 via Arâches to Les Carroz.

15 min from the motorway exit.

By train

Cluses station (nearest). From Geneva, the Léman Express (L3) reaches Cluses in 1h30. Tickets on sncf-connect.com.

Then the “Les Carroz Flaine Express” shuttle connects to the resort (booking required).

By plane

Geneva International Airport ≈ 45 min. Professional transfers to the resort: Les Carroz Transfert, Balme Transfert.

The ski area — Grand Massif

Les Carroz is part of the Grand Massif, France's 4th-largest linked ski area: 5 resorts (Flaine, Les Carroz, Morillon, Samoëns, Sixt), 265 km of slopes, 139 runs and 62 lifts, from 700 m to 2,500 m. The Carroz gondola, a 5-min walk away, gives direct access to the area.
