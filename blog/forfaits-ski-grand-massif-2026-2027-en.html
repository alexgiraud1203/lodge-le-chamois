Grand Massif ski pass 2026-2027: prices and opening dates

By Lodge Le Chamois · August 28, 2026

In late August the larches above Les Carroz have barely started to turn — and yet this is already the right moment to lock in your winter. The Grand Massif opening dates and 2026-2027 ski pass prices are out, and the best season-pass window of the year closes on 30 September. Here is what you need to know before booking.

Opening dates, resort by resort

The Grand Massif links five resorts — Flaine, Les Carroz, Morillon, Samoëns and Sixt — across 265 km of pistes between 720 and 2,500 m. Each opens at its own pace:

  • Flaine: 5 December 2026 to 18 April 2027 (the longest season, and the highest).
  • Les Carroz and Samoëns: 12 December 2026 to 11 April 2027.
  • Morillon: 19 December 2026 to 4 April 2027.
  • Sixt: 19 December 2026 to 7 March 2027.

In practice, if you are after a first snowy weekend in early December, Flaine opens the season — a 25-minute drive from Les Carroz, or ski-in once the link is running.

How much is a lift pass in 2026-2027?

Three options cover most stays:

  • Grand Massif pass (all five resorts): €63.00 a day for adults (15-74), €50.40 reduced (8-14). The daily rate is the same for 2 to 7 days, consecutive or not, valid throughout winter 2026-2027. A 4-hour option is available at €56.70.
  • Villages pass (Les Carroz, Morillon, Samoëns, Sixt, without Flaine): €58.50 a day for adults, €46.80 reduced. A sensible compromise if you stay on the tree-lined side.
  • Les Carroz beginner pass: €28 a day for adults, €21 reduced. It covers two return trips on the Kédeuze gondola, the Crêtes and Plein Soleil chairlifts and drag lifts, plus free use of the Figaro and Molliets drag lifts — ideal for a first family week.

Worth knowing: skiing is free for under-8s and for anyone aged 75 and over, on proof of age at sales outlets. Add €2 for the rechargeable hands-free card, which is non-refundable.

Season pass: the PREM'S window closes on 30 September

This is the real early-autumn bargain. The Grand Massif season pass gives unlimited access to all five resorts all winter, and the price depends on when you buy:

  • PREM'S rate, until 30 September 2026: €658 (26-74), €439 (8-25), €59 (under 8 and 75+).
  • PROMO rate, until 15 November 2026: €732 and €488.
  • Full price afterwards: €1,316 and €878.

In other words, exactly half price if you buy before the end of September. There is also a winter + summer version at €748 in PREM'S, which includes the summer lifts — worth considering if you plan to come back for the Bike Park.
