Skiing in January in Les Carroz: quiet slopes and 2027 tips

By Lodge Le Chamois · November 15, 2026

Between the end of the Christmas holidays and the start of the February break, there is a window that regulars know well: January. Lift queues shrink, groomed runs stay smooth for longer and the resort feels like a village again. Here is why January 2027 is a great time to ski from Les Carroz d'Arâches, and how to make the most of it.

A four-week window outside French school holidays

The 2026-2027 French school calendar makes this quiet period easy to spot. The Christmas holidays, shared by all three zones, end on Monday 4 January 2027. The winter holidays only start on Saturday 6 February 2027 for zone C (Paris, Montpellier, Toulouse), on 13 February for zone A and on 20 February for zone B.

In between, no French zone is on holiday. That leaves about four full weeks when the Grand Massif, with its 265 km of slopes and 62 lifts across Flaine, Les Carroz, Morillon, Samoëns and Sixt, is far less busy than during the holiday weeks.

What January changes on the slopes

  • Shorter lift queues: on weekdays you ride run after run instead of waiting, which adds up quickly over a ski day.
  • Better snow on the pistes: fewer skiers means groomed snow holds up later into the morning.
  • Easier ski lessons: outside the holidays, private lessons and convenient time slots are easier to book.
  • Deep-winter cold: January is often one of the coldest months in the mountains. Pack base layers, warm gloves and goggles, and enjoy bluebird days above the sea of clouds.

Prices designed for the quieter weeks

For winter 2026-2027, Les Carroz has announced several measures that favour off-peak stays. The 39 € Proximité pass is reserved for regional residents, Monday to Friday outside French school holidays, on the Les Carroz sector: if you live locally, January is exactly when it applies. For families, the Mini-8 scheme makes the day pass free for children under 8 and caps 2- to 7-day passes at 29 €.

Accommodation is also easier to book in January than at Christmas or in February: it is the right time to aim for an apartment with a terrace without planning a year ahead. Adult pass prices and opening dates are covered in our article Grand Massif ski passes 2026-2027.

Save the date: the Starlit Trek, 8 and 22 January 2027

January has its own highlight. The Les Carroz tourist office runs the Rando sous les étoiles (Starlit Trek) on Fridays 8 and 22 January 2027, 5 pm to 10 pm: a guided ski-touring climb at sunset from the bottom of the gondola, a Savoyard dinner at the mountain restaurant, then a torchlight descent.

  • Registration online only, no on-site sign-up.
  • Limited places (150 participants), beginners not accepted.
  • The 2026 edition sold out, with an early-bird price of 35 €. The 2027 price is still to be confirmed on the tourist office website: sign up early.
